# Code of Conduct

We want Git Wrapped to be a friendly place to learn, build and share, for people of every background and experience level.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.
- Give feedback that is specific and constructive, and accept it the same way.
- Assume good intentions, and say so when you are unsure.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing other people's private information
- Spam, and anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where people interact around the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use the repository's private reporting option under the **Security** tab or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
